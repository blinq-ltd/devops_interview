# Scenario 4 — Central observability, loss and cardinality

A request generator is continuously producing traffic and metrics for the
observability stack. Something about its telemetry is causing an
unexpectedly fast, unbounded growth in the number of active time series.

**Ideal end state**: the series growth is stopped at its source in the
collector configuration — without losing the ability to investigate any
individual request afterward. You'll also be asked to reason about, and in
some cases observe live, what happens to metrics and logs during a brief
telemetry delivery interruption.

## (a) Existing resources

- **Grafana**: `http://<this-workstation's-public-IP>:3000`. Admin
  password: `kubectl --namespace observability get secrets
  kube-prometheus-stack-grafana -o jsonpath="{.data.admin-password}" |
  base64 -d`.
- **Prometheus**: `http://<this-workstation's-public-IP>:9090`, already a
  Grafana datasource.
- **Loki**: already a Grafana datasource.
- **Alloy**: the collector, `observability` namespace, release name
  `alloy`. Its configuration is a Helm-managed ConfigMap, sourced from
  `interview/prereq/4/alloy-config.alloy` in this repo. To change it and
  apply:
  ```
  helm upgrade alloy grafana/alloy --version 1.13.1 -n observability \
    -f interview/prereq/4/alloy-values.yaml \
    --set-file alloy.configMap.content=interview/prereq/4/alloy-config.alloy
  kubectl rollout restart deployment/alloy -n observability
  ```
  (ConfigMap changes don't auto-restart the pod.)
- **`synthetic-generator`**: a Deployment in `observability`, the source
  of the traffic above.

## (b) Scenario tasks

Find what's driving the series growth, and fix it where it belongs in the
pipeline — without losing the ability to trace a specific request. Be
ready to discuss and, where asked live, observe what your telemetry
pipeline actually retains, replays, or loses when delivery is interrupted
briefly.

## (c) Final outputs and proof of completion

- A query/screenshot showing the series count for the affected metric
  before your fix (growing) and after (flat).
- The change you made, committed.
- Evidence you can still identify and investigate a specific request after
  the fix — show where that identifying information now lives and how you
  used it.
